## Tentative topics

Empirical risk minimisation and regularisation; PAC learning; VC dimension; kernel methods (SVM); least squares regression; deep learning.

## Teaching methods

Introductory online lectures (recorded and/or live), exercises with formative feedback, live sessions discussing assignments.

## Additional information

3 ECTS = 75 h: 20 h lectures, 10 h background material, 10 h exercises, 20 h coursework, 15 h final project.

## Lecturers

### Scope and examination, 2020W–2026W

Two rewrites, both important. See [../notes/00-exam-focus.md](../notes/00-exam-focus.md) §2.

| semesters | topic list | examination |
|---|---|---|
| 2020W, 2021S | Statistical learning theory; kernel-based learning; **online learning; clustering; semi-supervised learning** | "regularly submitted written coursework **and** a larger final project, such as a written report or a (recorded or online presented) talk" |
| 2022W – 2024S | **rewritten**: ERM and regularisation; PAC learning; VC dimension; kernel-based learning (SVM); least squares regression; deep learning | "regularly submitted written coursework, a practical project, and **a final discussion**" — no thresholds stated |
| 2024W – 2026W | unchanged | **rewritten**: "(1) mandatory and regularly submitted written coursework, (2) a practical project, and (3) **a final oral exam**", with ≥ 50 % on coursework+project combined **and** ≥ 50 % on the oral, oral repeatable once |

So the current topic list dates from **2022W** and the current examination rules
from **2024W**. Any description older than that is misleading; in particular the
"presentation of a research topic" option belongs to 2020W–2021S only.

### The 6 ECTS twin: 194.201

First offered **2025W** (2025S and earlier return HTTP 404) [S4]. The lecturers'
own course page links 194.100 and 194.201 side by side as the "3 ECTS version"
and the "6 ECTS version" of one lecture [S6], and VoWi files both numbers on a
single page [S8]. 194.201 repeats the six topics above and adds: *theory of deep
learning; deep learning architectures; graph neural networks; expressivity and
logics; interpretability, robustness, privacy, and fairness*. Its learning
outcomes are a superset, asking additionally to "rigorously prove key theoretical
results" and to "design and conduct small-scale research or application
projects". Workload 150 h rather than 75 h.
