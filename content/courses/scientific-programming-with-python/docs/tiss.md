### What the diff means

1. **The exam gained a multiple-choice part in 2024W** and has kept it. Before
   that it was programming exercises only, and in 2021W/2022W it was delivered
   as a TUWEL quiz or a Jupyter notebook under webcam invigilation. The 2026W
   wording is the 2024W wording verbatim.
2. **The lecture period shrank.** 2022W and 2023W ran the full semester
   (15–16 Tuesdays); 2024W onwards stops in early December — eight or nine
   slots. 2026W has exactly **eight** Tuesdays (13.10, 20.10, 27.10, 03.11,
   10.11, 17.11, 24.11, 01.12), matching the eight-lecture plan on the only
   public course homepage this lecture ever had (2019W, `[S8]`). The same
   syllabus is therefore delivered in roughly half the calendar time it had in
   2023W, with the exercises carrying the rest.
3. **The audience roughly doubled in 2024W**, when the course became mandatory
   in the 5th semester of the *Technische Informatik* bachelor (033 535)
   alongside CSE. A mandatory bachelor course with hundreds of students is the
   most plausible reason multiple choice appeared in the same year.
4. **Registration is a week earlier each year but the pattern is stable**:
   opens mid-September, closes on the Thursday before or of the first lecture
   week, deregistration one week after the lecture starts (2026W: 20.10).
5. **Nothing changed between 2025W and 2026W** except dates and the slot moving
   from 13:00 to 11:00 (already reflected above). No scope, ECTS, lecturer or
   examination change.
