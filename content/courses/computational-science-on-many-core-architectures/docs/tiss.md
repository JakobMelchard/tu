## Subject (as on TISS)

Amdahl's law; FLOPs, bandwidth and latency; performance modelling; GPUs (SIMT processing, thread-block synchronisation); programming models (annotation-driven such as OpenMP, native such as CUDA); FPGAs; emerging many-core architectures.

## Teaching methods

New material as prepared videos for weekly self-study; a one-hour interactive hybrid lecture each week for questions; hands-on exercises between lectures; short reports submitted by students; review and discussion of submissions.
