## Examination modalities

9 exercise assignments. Before each session students tick the solved problems on TUWEL and upload their solutions. Solutions are presented by volunteers and discussed. Uploaded solutions are graded; a missing, incomplete, copied or clearly AI-generated solution gives the sheet 0 points, and a second such case gives a negative course grade. **Half of the exercise points are required to sit an exam.** Grade: **20 % exercise sessions, 40 % midterm (mid November), 40 % final (end of January)**. The mean of the two partial exams must be positive. Each participant can retake one partial exam at the end of February.

## Literature and previous knowledge

Katz & Lindell, Introduction to Modern Cryptography, 3rd ed. (the lecture mainly follows it). No specific knowledge required; complexity theory and discrete mathematics help; mathematical maturity needed for security proofs. Typically taken in the 5th semester. Continuative: 192.115 VU Advanced Cryptography, 192.124 VU Symmetric Cryptography. Language: English.
