## Subject (as on TISS)

Three blocks: advanced techniques of processing relational data (procedural SQL, Common Table Expressions, recursive queries, Datalog); distributed data processing (MapReduce, Spark); principles of NoSQL systems.

## Teaching methods

Recordings on TUWEL; in-person teaching units as introduction, Q&A and summary of each block (also streamed and recorded); three exercises, one per block, solutions discussed with tutors (online via Zoom). Block-1 classes live in the first week of term.

## Lecturers

## Examination modalities

Three exercise sheets (15 %, 15 %, 10 %) with tutor interviews, and three written exams (20 % each, one per block). Positive: at least 30/60 from the written exams and at least 50/100 overall. One block's exam may optionally be repeated at the "bonus exam". Written exams in person in presumably two large lecture rooms; no late registrations by email.
