**What changed, and what did not.**

- **Scope: nothing changed.** The ten-item subject list, the learning outcomes,
  the teaching methods, the examination modalities and the literature line are
  byte-identical across all three years. So is the ECTS.
- **The teaching team changed completely for 2025W** and stayed that way for
  2026W. 2024W was an Institute-for-Microelectronics course; from 2025W it is
  co-taught by four institutes. The slot grew from two hours to three at the
  same time. Nothing public says which lecturer takes which of the ten topics.
- **Exam dates and exam format are unchanged in substance.** Note that TISS
  renders the *current* exam table on archived course pages: the 2024W and 2025W
  pages both show the 27.01.2027 and 05.03.2027 sittings, so historical exam
  dates cannot be recovered from TISS.
- **No change to dates, registration windows or exam format for 2026W** relative
  to what is already recorded here and in `_schedule/dates.json`. The 2026-09-21
  transcription above was re-checked field by field against the live page on
  2026-09-22 and is exact.
