Questions via TUWEL forum, protocol or aum@tuwien.ac.at; Matrix chat. **Compulsory final meeting** presenting the protocol (30-minute slot). Optional: facilitating meditations, written scientific work, review.

## Examination modalities

Points: 1 per short meditation, 2 per full meditation (about 1 h), 3 for participation/self-assessment/reviews, 4 per attended group meditation, 10 per meditation organised for a group, 25 for the written scientific work. Precondition for the final meeting: fully present at all meetings (signed sick notes accepted), fully prepared with at most one exception, every protocol submitted, **at least 100 points**. Grades: 4 at 100, 3 at 125, 2 at 150, 1 at 175.
