## Teaching methods

Slides with blackboard explanations, hardware/real-world demonstrations, exercises. Practical part: five exercises (packet traces, topology derivation, TCP behaviour scenarios) scheduled after lectures 7, 9, 16, 19, 22.

## Additional information

Requires access to a computer. ECTS breakdown: lectures 24 h, assignments 100 h (5 × 20 h), online discussion and self-study 22 h, midterm 2 h, final 2 h.

## Examination modalities

Midterm after lecture 14 (100 pts), final after lecture 24 (100 pts), five exercises (20 pts each). 150 pts to pass. Grades: sehr gut 240+, gut 211-240, befriedigend 181-210, genügend 150-180. Below 150 after at least two parts: optional oral exam with an external professor.
