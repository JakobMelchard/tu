## Subject (as on TISS)

Provable security and the random-oracle model; elliptic-curve-based cryptography; zero-knowledge and succinct proof systems; secure multi-party computation; lattice-based cryptography (quantum-secure public-key schemes).

## Teaching methods

Interactive lectures (slides/blackboard) and problem assignments with presentation and discussion in exercise sessions. Lectures recorded. Presence in the exercise sessions is **mandatory until 12:00** (2026S: 13 and 27 March, 24 April, 22 and 29 May, 19 June). Tutorial on provable security on 6 March 2026.

## Examination modalities

Homework assignments; solutions ticked and uploaded in TUWEL; in the exercise sessions a randomly sampled student among those who ticked presents. No attempt or not own work: points of that sheet withdrawn. Unexcused absence: the session's checkmarks withdrawn; a second unexcused absence: negative grade. Grade: 40 % exercise sessions (percentage ticked) + 60 % two partial exams (2026S: 8 May and 26 June); mean of the partial exams must be at least 50 %; two attempts at the final exam, the later counts; both parts must be positive.
