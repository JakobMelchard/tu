## Examination modalities

Exercises + an SQL exam during the semester + a written multiple-choice exam at the end. SQL exam: computer-assisted, in presence in the Informatiklabor, max 50 points, **at least half required**, two dates, up to two attempts, latest counts. MC exam: in presence in a lecture hall, all topics, max 100 points, **at least half required**, two dates, up to two attempts, latest counts.
