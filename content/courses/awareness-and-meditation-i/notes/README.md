# 064.004 Awareness and Meditation I — what the course actually requires

**There are no topic notes here, and there is no `src/`.** This course has
*mode of examination: immanent* [S1] — no written exam, no oral exam, no test,
no exercise sheet, no assignment with a right answer. It is assessed by
attendance, by a logged meditation practice, and by a 30-minute final meeting in
which the lecturer checks whether the log is true [S1]. Nothing here can be
prepared for by reading, so nothing has been written to read.

What *can* be got wrong is the administration, and it can be got wrong
irreversibly. This file is the checklist. Every statement is cited to
[`../refs/SOURCES.md`](../refs/SOURCES.md); where a fact is not public it is
marked **unknown until TUWEL opens**.

Sources verified 2026-09-22. TISS is edited during the term — re-read [S1]
before each deadline.

---

## 2. How the grade is computed

Two independent hurdles: a set of preconditions, then a point total. Missing any
precondition bars you from the final meeting, which ends the course — it is not
a bad grade, it is no grade.

### Preconditions for admission to the final meeting [S1]

| # | precondition | tolerance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | fully present at **all** meetings | none, except a **signed sick note in TUWEL** |
| 2 | fully prepared for **all** meetings | **exactly one** exception |
| 3 | **at least two** attended or organised meditations **per deadline** | none |
| 4 | each protocol submitted **within** its deadline | none |
| 5 | at least **100 points** | — |

Preconditions 3 and 4 are **new in 2026W**; 2025W required only "have submitted
each protocol" and set no per-deadline meditation floor [S2]. Precondition 1's
sick notes moved from the protocol to TUWEL [S2 → S1].

TISS is explicit about what counts as a partial achievement (*Teilleistung*),
which is what determines whether anything can be retaken [S1]:

- the **preparations** are the partial achievements — and "retakes are implied,
  because one preparation can be missing";
- the **protocol submissions** are not ("they only document the achievement");
- the **final meeting** is not ("it only verifies the partial achievements").

So the single tolerated missing preparation is the entire retake mechanism. There
is no second sitting.

Separately: **a certificate is issued only to students who did the entry
submission** (the Assignments document in TUWEL) [S1]. That is the first thing to
do after TUWEL opens on 01.10.2026.

### The point table [S1]

| points | for |
|---:|---|
| 1 | per **short** meditation logged (no fixed duration; the minimum rises from 5 min to 30 min over the term) |
| 2 | per **full** meditation logged (fixed instructions and duration, ≈ 1 h) |
| 3 | for participation, self-assessment or reviews |
| 4 | per **attended group** meditation logged |
| 10 | per meditation **organised for a group** |
| 25 | for the **written scientific work** |

All figures are "up to" [S1].

### Grades [S1]

| grade | points |
|---|---|
| 4 *Genügend* | ≥ 100 |
| 3 *Befriedigend* | ≥ 125 |
| 2 *Gut* | ≥ 150 |
| 1 *Sehr gut* | ≥ 175 |

### What the arithmetic implies

*Derived from the table above, not stated by TISS.* The ECTS breakdown budgets
**60 hours of meditation** [S1]. At 2 points per ≈ 1 h full meditation, 60 h buys
about **120 points** — comfortably past the 100-point pass, and comfortably short
of the 175 needed for a grade 1.

| route to 175 | meditation hours it costs |
|---|---|
| full meditations only (88 × 2) | ≈ 88 h — **~28 h over the budget** |
| written scientific work (25) + full meditations (75 × 2) | ≈ 75 h — still over |
| written work (25) + 5 organised meditations (50) + full meditations (50 × 2) | ≈ 50 h + organising — **inside the budget** |

So the two levers that actually move the grade are the **written scientific work**
(25 points, a quarter of the pass mark, for work that costs no meditation hours)
and **organising meditations** (10 points each — by far the best rate in the
table). Attending a group meditation pays 4 points; if one 2-hour
`roomTUmeditate` slot counts as one attended meditation, that is the same 2
points per hour as meditating alone, so group attendance is driven by
precondition 3 and by the course's purpose, not by the scoreboard. **Whether a
2-hour slot counts as one meditation or more is not stated in TISS — unknown.**

## 3. What you actually do, week to week

From the teaching-methods section [S1]:

- **Meetings**, Wed 10:00–12:00: questions, group work, polls. **Inverted
  classroom** — the TUWEL e-learning material is preparation *for* the meeting,
  and turning up unprepared burns your one tolerated exception.
- **Meditation**: in meetings, online, with guests, in groups, or alone. Group
  meditation is self-organised and has three steps — preparation and repeating of
  the instructions; the meditation as individual experience; sharing about the
  experience with self-inquiry.
- **Open meditation slots**, all in Theresianumgasse HS 1 – MWB [S1]:

  | slot | when |
  |---|---|
  | Morning Meditation | Wed 07:00–10:00 |
  | noon meditation | Wed 12:00–14:00 |
  | roomTUmeditate | Fri 07:00–09:00, Fri 18:00–20:00 |
  | roomTUmeditate | Sat 08:00–10:00, Sat 13:00–15:00 |

  Friday and Saturday slots run 02.10.2026 – 23.01.2027 weekly, with no Friday
  slot on 25.12.2026 or 01.01.2027; the Saturday slots run through the Christmas
  break [S1]. Expect extra one-off slots to be added during the term: 2025W
  gained a "PWHH!" evening, a 3-hour Friday session and an organised meditation
  [S2], and 2024W gained consultations and six organised meditations [S3].
- **Protocol**: logged continuously in **a web app built for it**, confirmed by
  regular deadlines, recording per meditation the date, time, which meditation,
  which group, and whether and how it was organised; plus a self-study of your
  own progress against criteria you choose [S1].
- **Feedback**: after each deadline, via TUWEL [S1].
- **Final meeting**: a self-chosen 30-minute slot after the last submission, in
  which you present the protocol and the lecturer checks the meditations really
  happened [S1].
- **Announcements**: in TISS before the first meeting, in TUWEL only afterwards
  [S1]. Watch the TISS page until 07.10.2026, then stop watching it. There is no
  third channel: the E064 Student Support overview does not mention the course,
  meditation or mindfulness at all [S11].
- **Questions**: TUWEL forums, the protocol, or `aum@tuwien.ac.at` [S1].

## 4. Preconditions on you, and what the course is not

TISS states: *no prior knowledge needed*, but **"Prerequisite is good physical
and mental condition. Otherwise contact `aum@tuwien.ac.at` before registration."**
[S1] "Potential risks while doing meditations" is an explicit topic of the course
[S1]. Take the contact instruction at face value; it is a registration-time
instruction, not a formality.

The course positions itself, in its own words [S1], as having nothing to do with
medical or psychological treatment or therapy; with belief, life coaching,
religion, esotericism or moral teachings; or with leading figures of any kind.

Language: German by default, **"if required in English"** [S1] — the title and
the TISS page exist in both; the German title is *Achtsamkeit und Meditation I*
[S5].
