## Teaching methods

Meditation in regular meetings, online, with guests, in groups or alone. Group
meditation is **self-organised** and has three steps: preparation and repeating
of the instructions; the meditation as individual experience; sharing about the
experience with self-inquiry.

Regular meetings answer questions and run group work and polls. **Inverted
classroom**: TUWEL e-learning material is the preparation for each meeting.

**Protocol**: students log meditations continuously **in a web app designed for
this purpose**, with regular deadlines (see `schedule.pdf`) by which the protocol
must be confirmed. Per meditation the protocol records date, time, which
meditation, which group, and whether and how it was organised; students also
conduct a self-study of their progress using self-chosen criteria or a scale.

Feedback and current scores after each deadline via TUWEL. Questions at any time
in TUWEL forums, in the protocol, or to `aum@tuwien.ac.at`. Social interaction in
the meetings and through a supervised TUWEL forum.

**Final meeting**: compulsory, after the last submission, in a self-chosen 30-minute
slot; students present the protocol and the lecturer checks whether the
meditations were actually done as written.

Optional: facilitating meditations (as part of the protocol **or instead of it**,
for Facilitators); a written scientific work about meditation using the literature
of current meditation research; reviews of scientific work.

## Additional information

Registration via TISS groups, then TUWEL. `schedule.pdf` lists all meetings with
compulsory attendance and preparation. Only register as a **Meditator** if there
is no scheduling conflict with any meeting in `schedule.pdf`. The final meeting
is a self-chosen 30-minute slot. Announcements before the first meeting in TISS,
afterwards only in TUWEL.

ECTS breakdown: 4.5 h preparation + 10.5 h meetings + 60 h meditation = 75 h.

Positioning: the course is for getting to know meditation and integrating it into
life, finding balance amid university life, and finding better ways to cope. It
has nothing to do with medical or psychological treatment or therapy; belief,
life coaching, religion, esotericism or moral teachings; or leading figures of any
kind.

## Examination modalities

A certificate is issued to every student who did the **entry submission** (see the
Assignments document in TUWEL). Scores, up to:

| points | for |
|---:|---|
| 1 | per short meditation logged (no fixed duration; minimum rises from 5 min to 30 min over the term) |
| 2 | per full meditation logged (fixed instructions and duration, ≈ 1 h) |
| 3 | for participation, self-assessment or reviews |
| 4 | per attended group meditation logged |
| 10 | per meditation organised for a group |
| 25 | for the written scientific work |

Precondition to be allowed to the final meeting:

1. fully present at **all** meetings (signed sick notes **in TUWEL** accepted);
2. fully prepared for all meetings, with **a maximum of one exception** (the
   preparations are the partial achievements of the course; retakes are implied
   because one preparation can be missing);
3. **at least two attended or organised meditations per deadline**;
4. each protocol submitted **within** the deadline;
5. at least **100 points**.

Grades after a successful final meeting: 4 ≥ 100, 3 ≥ 125, 2 ≥ 150, 1 ≥ 175.

Protocol submissions are not partial achievements (they only document the
achievement); the final meeting is not one either (it only verifies the partial
achievements).
