# Sources — 064.004 Awareness and Meditation I

Register of every source consulted for this course. `../notes` cites these as
`[S<n>]`. Retrieval dates are the day the page was fetched; TISS pages are
edited during the term, so re-check before a deadline.

**Vendoring policy.** Nothing is vendored and there is no `fetch-sources.sh`,
because there is nothing to fetch: this course names no literature, publishes no
script, slides or exercise sheet, and has no public PDF of any kind. Its only
document — `schedule.pdf` — lives in TUWEL behind a login and is not ours to
copy. See [`README.md`](README.md).

**Scope of this pass.** 064.004 is assessed *immanent* by attendance, a
meditation protocol and a points total. There is no written or oral exam, no
algorithm, no formula and no code. The pass therefore collected what the course
*requires of the student* rather than what it teaches; see
[`../notes/README.md`](../notes/README.md).

## Named literature: none

S1's Literature section reads, in full: **"No lecture notes are available."**
Same in S2, S3 and S4. The course names **no textbook, no article and no author**
anywhere on any of the four pages. The one place literature appears at all is the
optional written scientific work, which S1 describes only as using "the literature
of current meditation research" — with no reading list, no exemplar and no
minimum. Part 1.4 of this pass ("named literature") and Part 1.5 ("standards and
primary sources") therefore have no subject matter here.
