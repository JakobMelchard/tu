VU, 3.0 h, 4.5 ECTS, presence. TUWEL course available from 01.10.2026. Mode of examination: immanent.

## Teaching methods

Classroom lectures and exercises. Exercises apply ML techniques to data sets and implement ML algorithms; prepared at home and presented/discussed in class.

## Additional information

Held completely in TUWEL; all questions go to the TUWEL forum only. Apply to the TISS group, then follow the TUWEL link. ECTS breakdown: 13 classes 26 h, 2 presentation classes 8 h, assignments 46.5 h, exam 32 h, total 112.5 h. Note: the page still says "Preliminary talk: 2.10.2025, 16-18, EI3" (stale from 2025W); further schedule in TUWEL.

## Examination modalities

Exercises on ML experiments with a toolkit of choice (scikit-learn, Matlab, R, WEKA, ...); written exam at end of semester. **The weighting of the two components is not published** — the sibling course 192.183 states 50 % / 50 % [S4], but that is not a statement about this course.

**The interesting change is the curricula one.** 066 931, 066 937 and 066 926
moved to the new course **192.183 Machine Learning** (VU, 4.0 h, 6.0 ECTS,
Bellec/Musliu/Marty), which first ran in 2026S [S4]. 184.702 is now essentially
the Data Science course (066 645, mandatory, 1st semester) plus electives —
which is what it is for CSE. The two courses share Musliu and much of the
syllabus, but 192.183 adds learning theory and a far heavier deep-learning
component and is examined differently (50 % exam / 50 % projects). Exam material
dated 2026S that appears on the shared VoWi page belongs to **192.183, not to
this course** [S16]; see
[`../notes/00-exam-focus.md`](../notes/00-exam-focus.md).
