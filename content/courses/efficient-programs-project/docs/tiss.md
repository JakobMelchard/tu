## Subject

Groups of participants apply the contents of the VU "Efficient programs" on a program and present the results.

## Teaching methods

Work in a group on the project. Presentation of the results.

## Additional information

## Literature

No lecture notes are available. The course homepage (shared with the VU) describes the project: a group of 3–5 students optimises a program of its choice, presents which optimisation steps worked how well against expectations and, ideally, why. Algorithmic optimisations are allowed and usually expected; write the algorithmically improved version straightforwardly first, then apply measurable non-algorithmic steps. Results of earlier years are under `effizienz-abgaben/` (2025W: one group directory). Accounts on `g0.complang.tuwien.ac.at` are created in batches on Tuesdays.
