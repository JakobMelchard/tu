## Subject (as on TISS)

A number of modules of the VSC training and education programme; students select modules according to their needs and interests. Details and registration for each training event at https://vsc.ac.at/training.

## Teaching methods

Lecture; hands-on exercises on the VSC cluster: command line, JupyterHub and, if needed, virtual desktop for 3D rendering.

## Lecturers

Störi, Herbert; Blaas-Schenner, Claudia. Institute E057-09 Service Unit of ASC Research Center.

## Course dates

None in TISS; per training event on the VSC website.

## Examination modalities

Participation in the courses and review of the submitted program examples. A grade is given upon request. **Participation in at least 3 full training days is required.**

## Course registration

No TISS registration. Write to training@vsc.ac.at and register for the individual training events at https://vsc.ac.at/training.

## Curricula

ALG For all Students, not specified (free elective everywhere).

Language of instruction English; participation online or hybrid; details on the VSC website. Previous knowledge depends on the training event.
