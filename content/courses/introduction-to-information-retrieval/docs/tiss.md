## Subject (German on the page)

NLP basics: tokenization, stemming, normalisation, language models. IR basics: inverted index and search, scoring and ranking, relevance, evaluation metrics (MAP, NDCG), web search, search log analysis, PageRank, search UIs, statistical and explicit semantics, multimodal search (music, image).

## Teaching methods

Lectures on fundamentals (onsite); implementations in two practical exercises. TUWEL for dates, slides and exercise management.

## Additional information
