## Format (from the page)

Online: communication and materials via TUWEL and GitHub; YouTube uploads of recorded lectures (45–60 min each) with PDF slides and automatic captions; online office hours; exam with two dates. ECTS: lectures and reading 25 h (introduction, 3 crash-course IR, 3 NLP, 5 neural IR, special topics), exercises 45 h, exam 5 h.

## Teaching methods

Flipped classroom, remote teaching; participation in an international evaluation campaign with a competitive leaderboard; programming IR methods such as neural networks in PyTorch; critical assessment of scientific papers.
