## Subject (as on TISS)

1 Basic formalism: 1.1 pure and mixed states, Dirac notation, qubits, operators, trace, linear entropy, Bloch decomposition; 1.2 composite systems, tensor products, partial trace, generalised Bloch decomposition; 1.3 Shannon and von Neumann entropy, subadditivity, Araki-Lieb, concavity, relative entropy; 1.4 Schmidt decomposition and purification; 1.5 Hilbert-space geometry: overlap, Uhlmann fidelity and theorem, Bures and trace distance.
2 Quantum information processing: 2.1 EPR, Bell and CHSH inequalities, Tsirelson bound; 2.2 contextuality (Gleason, Kochen-Specker, Peres square, Mermin pentagram); 2.3 teleportation, entanglement swapping, dense coding; 2.4 BB84 and Ekert 91; 2.5 entanglement: separability, entropy of entanglement, mutual information, PPT criterion, witnesses; 2.6 channels: CPTP maps, Kraus, Stinespring, POVMs; 2.7 generalised measurements, Neumark dilation; 2.8 no-cloning, approximate cloning, no broadcasting, no deleting, distinguishing non-orthogonal states.
